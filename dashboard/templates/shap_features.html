Clinical Interpretation

MFCCs: Acoustic texture. Represents the spectral envelope and timbral characteristics of the breath sound.

Spectral Centroid: "Brightness" of the sound. Higher values often indicate sharper, hissier sounds like wheezes.

RMS Energy: Magnitude of the sound. Helps distinguish between silent phases and active breathing cycles.

ZCR: Zero-Crossing Rate. Measures the noise level or "hissiness" in the recording.

Note: Positive SHAP values (pointing right) increase the predicted risk score, while negative values (pointing left) decrease it.
