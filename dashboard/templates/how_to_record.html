How to Record

Follow these steps to capture high-quality respiratory audio samples suitable for AI analysis.

1
Prepare the Environment

Choose a quiet room with minimal background noise. Turn off fans, air conditioners, and other appliances. Ensure the patient is seated comfortably and relaxed.

2
Choose the Right Equipment

Use a digital stethoscope or a high-sensitivity microphone (≥16-bit, 22050 Hz sample rate). Place the microphone 2–5 cm from the chest wall. Avoid clothing friction.

3
Select Recording Locations

Record from 6–9 standard auscultation points: anterior (4 points) and posterior chest (4–5 points). Capture 2–4 full breathing cycles per position.

4
Instruct the Patient

Ask the patient to breathe slowly and deeply through their mouth. Avoid coughing or speaking during the recording. A single recording session should last 10–30 seconds.

5
Save as WAV Format

Export recordings in .wav format at a minimum of 22,050 Hz sample rate, mono channel. Avoid lossy compression (MP3, AAC) as it degrades subtle respiratory features.

6
Upload for Analysis

Navigate to Live Predict in the sidebar and drag-and-drop or browse your saved .wav file. The AI will extract 76 acoustic features and return a diagnosis within seconds.
